## How "direct output" is flagged

- **Direct**: generated by AI and committed with only mechanical fixes (lint, typos, failing-test fixes).
- **Edited**: AI draft that a team member changed in substance.
- **Human**: written or decided by a team member.

Every AI-assisted commit carries a `Co-Authored-By: Claude` trailer, and every pull request description ends with an "AI disclosure" section. `git log --grep "Co-Authored-By: Claude"` lists them.

## What the AI did not decide

- Business rules marked UNRESOLVED in the specification (cancellation, refunds, failed delivery, real payment provider) were **not** invented.
- Withdrawing A-13 and adding FR-31 was a team decision, recorded in the backlog and in PR #7.
- Limiting FoodGO to Lac 3 / Zone Industrielle Le Kram (FR-32, BR-17) was a team decision. So is the rule that restaurants outside the area cannot join. The open points that remain (delivery windows, how to measure traffic) are listed in ADR-0007, not decided by the AI.
- The team list, the repository, and the choice to submit were the team's.
